Orders · same chat thread

WhatsApp order automation for MSMEs — from chats to a clean register.

This does not replace WhatsApp. It reads the chats, voice notes and photos you already get, writes a structured register, and sends the confirmation back on the same thread.

WhatsApp · MSME orders7 Sep 2026 · Meet Deshani

In a Surat trading office or a loom unit, the order is rarely a clean purchase order. It is a voice note at 9.40 pm, a photo of a handwritten chit, or a three-line text with a design number and a “same as last”. Someone retypes that into Excel or Tally — late, and with mistakes that only show up at dispatch.

WhatsApp order automation does not ask customers to change. It watches the thread they already use, pulls a structured line, and sends a confirmation back on that same chat so the broker can say “yes” or correct it before the floor starts.

// why WhatsApp

Customers already message there

You cannot move a grey trader or a job-work agent onto a “dealer portal”. They will keep sending the order on WhatsApp. So the system has to live where the order already is — the same rule we use across AI automation for Indian MSMEs.

In textiles this is usually the first workflow that pays. We mapped it as workflow 01 in AI for the textile industry in Surat. The same pattern holds for any MSME whose book of orders is a WhatsApp inbox.

// the workflow

Watch → parse → write → confirm → flag exceptions

01 · watch

The numbers you already use

Business WhatsApp, the group with brokers, the personal line the owner still answers. No new number unless you want one.

02 · parse

Text, voice, photo

Party, item, qty, rate, delivery — pulled from the message as it arrived, including Gujarati, Hindi and Hinglish.

03 · write

A register your office can trust

One row per order, in the sheet or system you already open in the morning. Not a new CRM nobody logs into.

04 · confirm

Back on the same thread

“2 taka, design 4418, ₹X, delivery Friday — confirm?” The broker replies on WhatsApp. The register updates.

05 · exceptions

Ambiguous orders get flagged

Missing rate, two possible parties, a blurry chit — those go to a human. We would rather hold a line than invent a qty.

// the register

Fields that actually get used

FieldWhat we storeWhy it matters
PartyMatched name / WhatsApp numberStops “which Rameshbhai?” at dispatch
ItemQuality, design no., SKU, or the words they usedThe floor can cut or pick without calling back
QtyTaka, kg, pieces — as orderedThe usual source of silent mistakes

// the payoff

What changes in the first month

  • Fewer missed orders. A voice note at night is still a row in the morning book.
  • Fewer mistakes. Qty and design get confirmed before the floor starts.
  • Faster confirmations. The broker gets a clean recap on the same thread, not a call at 11.
  • A daily order book. Owners stop reconstructing the day from chat search.

// private + languages

On-prem, in the language the chat is in

Order chats are customer lists, rates and often designs. That is why this runs as private AI on machines you own — Gujarati, Hindi, English and Hinglish in, structured register out. Nothing trains a third-party system.

// refuse + rollout

We will say no. Then we start small.

If WhatsApp is not how orders actually arrive, we will tell you. A chatbot bolted onto a process that already works is a waste of your money and our week.

When it is the right problem, rollout looks like the rest of our work: one number or one broker group, a week of live samples, confirmation templates your owner signs off, then expand. Same method as Tally bill booking — draft, human approve, then scale.

// questions owners ask

FAQ

Does this replace WhatsApp?

No. Customers keep messaging the same number. The AI reads the thread and writes a register. Confirmations go back on that same chat. Your team does not move buyers onto a new app.

What about voice notes and photos of chits?

Those are the point. A lot of MSME orders never arrive as a typed line. The workflow is built for texts, voice notes and photos — and it flags the ones it cannot parse instead of inventing a qty.

Will it work in Gujarati, Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. Brokers and customers already mix languages on WhatsApp. The register can store a clean English/Gujarati line and still reply in the language the thread is using.

Is the chat history leaving my phone?

The deployment is private AI on hardware you own. Order text does not train a public model and does not need to sit in a foreign cloud. Same rule as owning the model.

Who is this not for?

If every order already lands as a clean PO in a system your team trusts, you may not need this. We refuse work where WhatsApp is not actually how orders arrive — we would rather say no than bolt a chatbot onto a process that is already fine.
