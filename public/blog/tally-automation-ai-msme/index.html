Munshi · Tally on your machines

Tally automation with AI — bills in, vouchers booked.

AI reads the bill, proposes the voucher, drops a draft in Tally, and your accountant approves. We do not replace Tally. We stop the retyping.

Tally · MSME accounts7 Sep 2026 · Meet Deshani

The bill arrives as a PDF, a photo, or a WhatsApp forward. Someone in accounts retypes it into Tally — party, GSTIN, HSN, qty, rate — and hopes the challan matches. At month-end the pile is still there.

The workflow we deploy is narrower than the pitch you hear from “AI accounting” tools. AI reads the bill. It proposes a voucher. Tally gets a draft. Your accountant approves. We do not replace Tally, and we do not auto-post into a live book.

// what breaks today

Where the hours actually go

  • PDF, photo and WhatsApp bills — three formats, one retype job, every day.
  • Challan mismatch — job-work and trading offices spend the afternoon hunting the right inward number.
  • GST typos — a wrong HSN or tax rate that only shows up when the return is being filed.
  • Month-end backlog — the books close late because entry never kept up with the inbox.

// the flow

Capture → Read → Match → Book draft → Approve

01 · capture

The bill as it already arrives

Email PDF, phone photo, WhatsApp forward. Nobody scans into a new portal.

02 · read

AI pulls the fields

Party, GSTIN, line items, qty, rate, tax. Gujarati and Hindi stamps stay in the record, not in a “could not read” pile.

03 · match

Challan and party against your books

The draft is tied to the inward or job-work challan where one exists. Exceptions get flagged, not quietly guessed.

04 · book draft

A voucher waiting in Tally

Your accountant sees a draft they already know how to review — not a new screen to learn.

05 · approve

A human still owns the books

Nothing is live until someone who knows the ledger says yes.

// questions owners ask

FAQ

Can it read Gujarati or Hindi stamps and bill text?

Yes. A large share of the bills we see from Gujarat traders and job-workers have Gujarati or Hindi stamps, item names and notes. Those stay in the draft for your accountant — they are not skipped just because they are not in English.

What if the AI misreads a bill?

Misreads stay a draft. Nothing posts to Tally until your accountant approves. The point is to stop retyping, not to remove the person who knows when a GST rate looks wrong.

Do I need new hardware?

Usually no. Ordinary office machines are enough for bill reading and draft booking. The workflow is private by default — ledgers stay on-prem. See private AI for Indian MSMEs.

Is this only for textile units?

No. Textile is where we see a lot of job-work bills, but trading, manufacturing and services offices have the same pile: PDF bills, WhatsApp photos, challans that do not match, and a month-end backlog.
