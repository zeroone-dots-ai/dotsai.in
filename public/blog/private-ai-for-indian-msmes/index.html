Private AI for Indian MSMEs — own it, don't rent it.

Most “AI for business” pitches end the same way: another login, another monthly bill, and your data sitting on someone else's servers. For an MSME in Surat, Rajkot or anywhere in Gujarat, that is a bad deal twice over.

You pay forever, and your designs, rates, ledgers and customer lists leave the building. Private AI flips that. The model runs on computers you already own. Nothing trains a third-party system. It keeps working when the internet does not. Once it is built, it is yours — like any other machine on your floor.

No jargon

What “private AI” actually means

Private AI is a model trained or configured for your work, deployed on your hardware. Your team keeps using WhatsApp, Tally, Excel, CRM or Slack — not a new chat wrapper and not a seat-based SaaS. ZeroOne D.O.T.S AI default: Own Your AI · Don't Rent It. That is the whole private AI models offer.

Why MSMEs should care

Your edge is the data

Rate lists, designs, ledgers, who pays on time — that is the business. Renting a cloud model means that edge sits on someone else's servers, and the bill climbs with every extra user or token. Cost stops climbing. Outages stop mattering. DPDP Act 2023 is simpler on-prem because customer lists never leave the building.

Where it pays first

We do not start with a “platform”. We start with the workflow that eats the most hours — reporting, Tally / Munshi, WhatsApp orders, follow-ups and credit. See workflow automation services and ready-made products.

Four steps. We stay until it works.

Sit inside the unit → fix one workflow → deploy on machines you own → stay until the team actually opens it on a Monday morning. That is the whole method.

Questions owners ask

Is private AI only for big IT companies?

No. The MSMEs we work with in Surat and across Gujarat already have computers, Tally, WhatsApp and a team that knows the work. Private AI sits on that — you do not need an IT department or a data-science bench.

Do I need a data centre?

No. Most MSME deployments run on machines you already own — a workstation, a small office server, or a VPS you control. The point is the model lives on hardware you own, not on a rented cloud login.

Will my team have to learn a new app?

Usually not. The default is WhatsApp, Tally, Excel, CRM or Slack — the tools they already open every morning. We do not sell a new chat wrapper or a per-seat SaaS login.

Does it work in Hindi and Gujarati?

Yes. Orders, bills, reports and reminders can run in Gujarati, Hindi, English or Hinglish — whatever your floor, brokers and customers actually use.
